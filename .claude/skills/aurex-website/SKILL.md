# Aurex Website Orchestrator

Use this skill as the top-level operating workflow for a complete Aurex website project.

## First rule

Do not jump into implementation because a client name, logo, or current website is available. Establish enough strategy and creative direction to make the build intentional.

Read these foundation files before major decisions:

- `FOUNDATION/AUREX-PRINCIPLES.md`
- `FOUNDATION/PROJECT-WORKFLOW.md`
- `FOUNDATION/CREATIVE-CONCEPT-METHOD.md`
- `FOUNDATION/LEAD-CONVERSION-STANDARD.md` when lead generation is relevant
- the active project brief and any approved strategy documents

## Operating sequence

1. Intake and brief
2. Discovery and research
3. Positioning and conversion strategy
4. Site architecture
5. Creative direction
6. Design system and homepage structure
7. Hero visual proof
8. Homepage build
9. Page-category bundles
10. Content and SEO depth
11. Conversion review
12. Motion and interaction pass
13. Responsive and accessibility QA
14. Technical and analytics QA
15. Final creative review
16. Launch readiness
17. Post-launch learning

Use the dedicated Aurex skills for each specialist stage instead of improvising a new process.

## Collaboration behavior

Operate autonomously inside an approved direction. Escalate decisions when they are highly subjective, expensive to reverse, strategically material, evidence is weak or conflicting, or multiple strong creative directions remain viable.

When escalating, provide:

1. the recommended choice
2. why it is recommended
3. strongest alternatives
4. tradeoffs
5. the exact decision required from the human creative lead

Do not ask for approval on routine implementation details.

## Project state

At the start of a session, determine the current project stage from existing files and code. Do not restart discovery or redo approved work without a reason.

Maintain clear project artifacts for major stages. Recommended documents include:

- `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`
- `DISCOVERY.md`
- `WEBSITE-STRATEGY.md`
- `SITE-ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md`
- `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`
- page specs as needed
- `QA-REPORT.md`
- `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`

## Quality control

Do not equate completion with code generation.

Before calling a major stage complete, verify the output against the approved business objective, conversion strategy, creative concept, responsive behavior, accessibility, SEO intent, performance implications, and actual browser experience.

When evidence is required, research current primary documentation or reputable research instead of relying on folklore or stale memory.

## Anti-rush rule

If the site looks generic, repetitive, weakly branded, poorly converted, visually unfinished, or technically fragile, continue iterating. Do not declare success because the requested files exist.
