# Aurex Art Direction

Read `FOUNDATION/CREATIVE-CONCEPT-METHOD.md` before producing directions.

## Purpose

Find a concept that belongs to this business and can organize the visual experience without weakening clarity or conversion.

Do not begin with a trend label such as "modern luxury," "glassmorphism," or "Awwwards style." Those may become execution tools, not the idea.

## Process

1. Re-read discovery, positioning, audience, proof, brand assets, logo, imagery, and physical/business details.
2. Identify 5-10 concept seeds rooted in real business truths.
3. Test seeds for specificity, story, conversion compatibility, scalability, and restraint.
4. Develop the strongest three into meaningfully different creative directions.
5. Recommend one with conviction.

## Each direction must define

- concept name
- business truth and customer transformation behind it
- narrative idea
- visual thesis
- color logic and section atmosphere
- typography personality and hierarchy
- image/video treatment
- layout and composition philosophy
- shapes, masks, surfaces, texture, and depth
- signature interaction or motion moments
- section-transition language
- CTA and form integration
- mobile translation
- asset requirements
- risks and anti-gimmick guardrails

## Visual reference behavior

Use references to extract principles, not to clone layouts. Explain exactly what is being borrowed conceptually, such as pacing, image scale, contrast, typography hierarchy, or interaction behavior.

## Premium test

Ask whether the direction feels considered in both obvious and subtle details. Avoid generic white-section stacks, repeated card grids, arbitrary gradients, excessive rounded boxes, and trendy effects with no connection to the client.

## Output

Create or update `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md` with:

1. creative opportunity summary
2. concept seeds considered
3. three developed directions
4. recommended direction and rationale
5. rejected risks and patterns
6. required imagery/video/visual assets
7. decision required from human creative lead

Do not implement the full site until the direction is approved.
