# Aurex SEO

SEO is part of the product architecture, not a keyword pass at the end.

## Research rule

For material or time-sensitive SEO recommendations, verify current search-engine documentation and strong primary sources. Do not rely on remembered algorithm folklore or arbitrary SEO-tool scores.

## Evaluate

- search intent and page purpose
- information architecture
- topical and service coverage
- local intent where relevant
- titles and descriptions
- heading structure
- semantic HTML
- content usefulness and specificity
- internal linking
- crawlability and indexability
- canonicals
- redirects and preservation of valuable existing URLs
- XML sitemap and robots behavior
- structured data only when valid and supported
- image/media discoverability where relevant
- performance and Core Web Vitals implications
- duplicate/thin content risk
- business/entity consistency for local sites

## Content standard

Do not keyword-stuff. Do not manufacture city/service pages that offer no distinct value. Do not force exact-match phrases into unnatural copy.

A page should exist because it satisfies a useful intent, supports the customer journey, creates meaningful topical depth, or fulfills a legitimate business purpose.

## Local business sites

When local search matters, verify business name, address, phone, service areas, location details, local proof, relevant services, and connections to the real-world business. Keep facts accurate and consistent.

## Output

Create or update `SEO-PLAN.md` with:

1. organic opportunity summary
2. priority intent clusters
3. page-to-intent map
4. content gaps
5. internal linking strategy
6. local SEO requirements where relevant
7. technical requirements
8. migration/redirect requirements
9. structured data plan
10. measurement plan
11. risks and unsupported assumptions

## Final audit

Inspect representative rendered pages and the production build where possible. Confirm that implementation matches the plan, not merely that metadata fields exist.
